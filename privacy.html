Privacy

What we keep, and why

The short version: if you sign up, we keep your email address so we can let you know when the camera launches and occasionally share updates beforehand. That’s it.

What we collect

When you sign up through this site, we store:

  • your email address
  • the form you signed up through
  • the date and time you signed up

We don’t ask for your name or any other personal details.

Why we collect it

We use your email address to let you know when the camera launches and, from time to time, to share relevant updates about .yesterday before launch.

We don’t sell, rent, or share your email address with other companies for their own marketing.

Where your information is stored

Your email address is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers located in Sydney, Australia.

This site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard technical and server logs as part of operating the site.

We also use Google Fonts, which means your browser may connect to Google to load the fonts used on the website.

These services help us run the site. We don’t provide your email address to them for advertising or marketing purposes.

Cookies and tracking

We don’t use advertising trackers or behavioural tracking on this site.

If that changes in the future, we’ll update this page.

Leaving the list

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in our emails.

You can also ask us to delete your email address by contacting [CONTACT EMAIL]. We’ll remove it as soon as reasonably possible.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

If the camera launch is cancelled and the mailing list is no longer needed, we’ll delete the list.

Who we are

.yesterday is an independent camera brand designed in Australia.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Australian privacy laws.
