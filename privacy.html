Privacy

What we keep, and why.

Short version: if you sign up, we keep your email address so we can tell you when the camera launches. That’s it.

What we collect

When you sign up on this site we store three things: your email address, which form you used, and the date and time you signed up. We don’t ask for your name or anything else.

Why we collect it

To send you one email on launch day, and occasionally news about the camera before then. We don’t sell, rent or share your address with anyone for their own marketing.

Where it’s stored

Your address is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in Sydney, Australia. This site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those files from Google when the page loads. None of these providers use your email for their own purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Getting off the list

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to delete your address at any time by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL], and we’ll remove it within a few days.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If the launch is called off, we’ll delete the whole list.

Who we are

.yesterday is a small camera brand designed in Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Act. Questions go to [CONTACT EMAIL].
